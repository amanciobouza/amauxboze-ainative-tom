## Governance Principles

1. Founder approval is required for consequential external commitments until explicitly delegated.
2. Agents may prepare, recommend, draft, classify, summarize, and execute low-risk internal actions.
3. Technical authority does not imply business authority.
4. A workflow may automate coordination, but may not silently bypass approval rights.
5. Any future delegation of decision rights must be explicit and documented.
