# Market Intelligence → Strategic Insight

## Purpose

Continuously transform external observations into evidence-backed insights that may affect Amaux Bozé product, brand, commerce, or operating decisions.

## Process Owner
**Nora — Head of Market Intelligence**

## Participating Agents
- Nora — research and synthesis
- Elena — prioritization / decision preparation
- Lucien — product interpretation
- Élodie — brand interpretation
- Marc — commercial interpretation
- Sophie — customer interpretation
- Amancio — strategic decision

## Workflow

### 1. Research Question or Watch Topic
Triggered by:
- Founder question
- Scheduled research
- Agent-detected change
- Product / launch workflow

Owner: Nora

### 2. Evidence Collection
Owner: Nora

Requirements:
- Prefer primary sources
- Record source and date
- Separate fact from interpretation
- Avoid unsupported market narratives

### 3. Observation Capture
Owner: Nora

Each observation records:
- What changed / was observed
- Source
- Date
- Geography / market
- Confidence
- Relevance tags

### 4. Pattern / Change Detection
Owner: Nora

Outputs:
- Repeated signals
- Emerging trend hypothesis
- Contradicting evidence
- Unknowns

### 5. Functional Interpretation

Lucien:
- product implications

Élodie:
- brand / cultural implications

Marc:
- commercial implications

Sophie:
- customer implications

### 6. Strategic Synthesis
Owners:
- Nora — evidence synthesis
- Elena — decision framing

Outputs:
- Insight
- Why it matters
- What is known
- What is uncertain
- Options
- Recommended question or next action

### 7. Founder Decision
Owner: Amancio

Possible outcomes:
- Act
- Investigate further
- Monitor
- Ignore / archive

### 8. Knowledge Update
Owner: Nora

Store:
- Observation history
- Insight
- Decision
- Follow-up condition
- Related products / initiatives

## State Model

```text
QUESTION
→ EVIDENCE_COLLECTED
→ OBSERVATIONS_RECORDED
→ PATTERN_ANALYZED
→ FUNCTION_INTERPRETED
→ INSIGHT_READY
→ FOUNDER_DECISION
→ KNOWLEDGE_UPDATED
```

## Principle

Observations and insights must remain separate. A source-backed fact should not be overwritten by later interpretation; instead, synthesis builds on the observation history.
