# Customer Feedback → Learning

## Purpose

Turn customer messages, reviews, support interactions, and community signals into structured insights and actionable organizational learning.

## Process Owner
**Sophie — Head of Customer & Community**

## Participating Agents
- Sophie — intake, classification, customer context
- Nora — pattern and evidence analysis
- Lucien — product implications
- Élodie — brand implications
- Marc — commerce implications
- Maya — communication/content implications
- Elena — prioritization and escalation

## Workflow

### 1. Intake
Owner: Sophie

Sources:
- Customer messages
- Reviews
- Social comments
- Support cases
- Post-purchase feedback

### 2. Classification
Owner: Sophie

Categories:
- Product quality
- Design
- Fit / comfort
- Shipping
- Pricing
- Communication
- Brand perception
- Website / checkout
- Support
- Other

### 3. Immediate Response Need
Owner: Sophie

If customer response is required:
- Draft response
- Escalate if consequential
- Send only within approved permission level

### 4. Pattern Detection
Owner: Nora

Outputs:
- Recurring themes
- Signal strength
- Supporting evidence
- Novel vs known issue

### 5. Functional Routing

Product issue → Lucien  
Brand issue → Élodie  
Commerce issue → Marc  
Content issue → Maya

Each owner provides:
- Interpretation
- Impact
- Proposed action

### 6. Prioritization
Owner: Elena

Criteria:
- Customer harm / friction
- Frequency
- Strategic relevance
- Revenue impact
- Brand impact
- Effort

### 7. Founder Gate
Owner: Amancio

Required when the proposed action changes:
- Product specification
- Pricing
- Brand principles
- Public commitments
- Material operating policy

### 8. Learning Capture
Owner: Sophie

Store:
- Signal
- Pattern
- Decision
- Action
- Outcome when known

## State Model

```text
RECEIVED
→ CLASSIFIED
→ RESPONDED_OR_ROUTED
→ ANALYZED
→ FUNCTION_REVIEWED
→ PRIORITIZED
→ ACTIONED
→ LEARNING_CAPTURED
```

## Principle

Feedback is not automatically truth. Individual feedback remains an observation until evidence or repeated patterns justify stronger conclusions.
