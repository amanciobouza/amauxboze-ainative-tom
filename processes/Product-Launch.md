# Product Launch

## Purpose

Bring an approved Amaux Bozé watch from final specification to a coordinated commercial launch across Shopify, content channels, and community touchpoints.

## Process Owner
**Marc — Head of Growth & Commerce**

## Final Approver
**Amancio — Founder**

## Participating Agents
- Elena — launch coordination
- Lucien — product accuracy
- Élodie — narrative and messaging
- Marc — commerce and launch mechanics
- Maya — content production
- Sophie — customer/community readiness
- Nora — market context
- Kai — integrations and automation support

## Workflow

### 1. Launch Brief
Owner: Elena

Inputs:
- Approved product specification
- Production timing
- Commercial objectives
- Target launch window

Outputs:
- Launch brief
- Milestones
- Dependencies
- Owners
- Open decisions

### 2. Product Truth Pack
Owner: Lucien

Outputs:
- Approved specifications
- Claims that are allowed
- Claims that are prohibited or unverified
- Product imagery requirements
- Variant / SKU information

### 3. Narrative & Messaging
Owner: Élodie

Outputs:
- Core message
- Product story
- Naming usage
- Message hierarchy
- Campaign narrative

### 4. Commercial Setup
Owner: Marc

Outputs:
- Price recommendation
- Shopify product draft
- Offer structure
- Shipping assumptions
- Inventory / availability logic
- Conversion plan

Gate:
Final price requires founder approval.

### 5. Content Production
Owner: Maya

Outputs:
- TikTok concepts/scripts
- Instagram assets/copy
- Pinterest assets/copy
- X posts
- Content calendar
- Repurposing plan

### 6. Customer & Community Readiness
Owner: Sophie

Outputs:
- FAQ
- Support guidance
- Expected objections/questions
- Review-response guidance

### 7. Market Context Check
Owner: Nora

Outputs:
- Last-minute competitor / market changes
- Relevant external timing risks
- Evidence-backed launch notes

### 8. Integrated Launch Review
Owner: Elena

Outputs:
- Launch readiness report
- Blockers
- Required approvals
- Go / revise recommendation

### 9. Founder Gate
Owner: Amancio

Possible outcomes:
- Go
- Revise
- Hold
- Cancel

### 10. Publish & Activate
Owners:
- Marc — Shopify
- Maya — social/content
- Sophie — community/support

Rule:
External publication requires explicit approval until delegated.

### 11. Post-Launch Learning
Owners:
- Marc — commerce performance
- Maya — content performance
- Sophie — customer signals
- Nora — external context
- Elena — consolidated review

Outputs:
- Launch review
- Learnings
- Recommended changes

## State Model

```text
PLANNED
→ PRODUCT_READY
→ MESSAGE_READY
→ COMMERCE_READY
→ CONTENT_READY
→ SUPPORT_READY
→ LAUNCH_REVIEW
→ APPROVED
→ LIVE
→ LEARNING
→ COMPLETE
```

Alternative states:
- ON_HOLD
- REVISION_REQUIRED
- CANCELLED

## Required Artifacts
- Launch Brief
- Product Truth Pack
- Messaging Framework
- Shopify Draft
- Content Plan
- Customer FAQ
- Launch Readiness Report
- Founder Approval
- Post-Launch Review
