# Content Campaign

## Purpose

Turn a brand, product, founder, or community objective into coherent multi-channel content that fits Amaux Bozé's voice and drives a defined outcome.

## Process Owner
**Maya — Head of Content & Social**

## Final Approver
**Amancio — Founder**

## Participating Agents
- Élodie — message and brand fit
- Maya — channel-native execution
- Marc — commercial objective / conversion
- Sophie — audience and customer perspective
- Nora — evidence / external context
- Elena — prioritization and coordination

## Workflow

### 1. Campaign Objective
Owner: Elena or Amancio

Define:
- Desired outcome
- Audience
- Product / theme
- Timing
- Constraints

### 2. Message Architecture
Owner: Élodie

Outputs:
- Core message
- Supporting messages
- Narrative frame
- Tone constraints

### 3. Audience & Evidence Check
Owners:
- Sophie — audience relevance
- Nora — factual support / market context

Outputs:
- Audience signals
- Evidence pack
- Risks / misunderstandings

### 4. Channel Strategy
Owner: Maya

Outputs:
- Channels
- Formats
- Cadence
- Content angles
- Repurposing logic

### 5. Commercial Alignment
Owner: Marc

Outputs:
- CTA
- Landing destination
- Conversion logic
- Tracking requirements

### 6. Content Production
Owner: Maya

Outputs:
- Draft assets / copy
- Scripts
- Captions
- Publishing schedule

### 7. Brand Review
Owner: Élodie

Checks:
- Tone
- Narrative coherence
- Claims
- Brand dilution
- Repetition / cliché risk

### 8. Founder Gate
Owner: Amancio

Possible outcomes:
- Approve
- Revise
- Reject

### 9. Publish
Owner: Maya

Rule:
Publishing remains approval-gated until explicitly delegated.

### 10. Performance Review
Owners:
- Maya — engagement
- Marc — commercial performance
- Sophie — qualitative response
- Nora — external/contextual interpretation

### 11. Learning Capture
Owner: Elena

Outputs:
- What worked
- What did not
- Reusable insight
- Recommended next experiment

## State Model

```text
OBJECTIVE_SET
→ MESSAGE_READY
→ AUDIENCE_CHECKED
→ CHANNEL_PLAN_READY
→ CONTENT_DRAFTED
→ BRAND_REVIEWED
→ APPROVED
→ PUBLISHED
→ MEASURED
→ LEARNED
```
