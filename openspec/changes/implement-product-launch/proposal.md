# Proposal

## Why

Amaux Bozé has a defined Product Launch business process, but it is not yet executable as a coordinated AI-native workflow.

After New Watch Development produces an approved specification, Product Launch must transform that approved product into a launch-ready commercial package while preserving product truth, brand coherence, customer readiness, and founder approval.

## What Changes

Implement Product Launch as a LangGraph workflow covering:

1. Launch Brief
2. Product Truth Pack
3. Narrative & Messaging
4. Commercial Setup
5. Content Production
6. Customer & Community Readiness
7. Market Context Check
8. Integrated Launch Review
9. Founder Gate
10. Publish & Activate
11. Post-Launch Learning

## Capabilities

### New Capability

- **product-launch** — coordinate the end-to-end preparation, approval, activation, and learning loop for an Amaux Bozé product launch.

## Dependencies

This change depends on:
- AI operating platform foundation
- Agent Registry
- Skill Registry
- Obsidian Adapter
- Model Router
- Approval Gates
- Workflow Runtime
- approved product specification from New Watch Development

V1 will prepare and approval-gate launch actions. Live publishing integrations may remain mocked until channel-specific tools are connected and approved.
