# Proposal

## Why

Amaux Bozé has a defined Content Campaign business process, but it is not yet executable as an AI-native workflow.

The workflow must turn a campaign objective into channel-native content while preserving message clarity, brand coherence, factual correctness, conversion intent, founder approval, and post-campaign learning.

## What Changes

Implement Content Campaign as a LangGraph workflow covering:

1. Campaign Objective
2. Message Architecture
3. Audience & Evidence Check
4. Channel Strategy
5. Commercial Alignment
6. Content Production
7. Brand Review
8. Founder Gate
9. Publish
10. Performance Review
11. Learning Capture

## Capabilities

### New Capability

- **content-campaign** — coordinate campaign planning, content production, approval, publishing, and learning.

## Dependencies

This change depends on the AI operating platform foundation and existing agent/skill authorization infrastructure.

The workflow will coordinate Elena, Élodie, Sophie, Nora, Maya, Marc, and Amancio.

V1 will keep publishing behind explicit founder approval and tool permissions.
