# Proposal

## Why

Amaux Bozé has defined New Watch Development as a core business process, but it is not yet executable as a coordinated multi-agent workflow.

The first business workflow should prove that the AI-native operating model can coordinate multiple specialized roles, preserve evidence and decisions, route work through explicit stages, pause for founder approval, and write durable knowledge back to Obsidian.

## What Changes

Implement the New Watch Development process as a LangGraph workflow covering:

1. Intent Definition
2. Evidence Gathering
3. Product Concept
4. Brand & Narrative Review
5. Commercial Review
6. Customer Perspective
7. Integrated Recommendation
8. Founder Gate
9. Final Product Specification
10. Production Approval

## Capabilities

### New Capability

- **new-watch-development** — orchestrate the end-to-end creation and approval of a new Amaux Bozé watch concept.

### Dependencies

This change depends on the AI operating platform foundation:
- Agent Registry
- Skill Registry
- Obsidian Adapter
- Model Router
- Approval Gates
- Workflow Runtime

## Impact

The workflow will coordinate Elena, Nora, Lucien, Élodie, Marc, Sophie, and Amancio.

It will not yet:
- place supplier orders
- approve production automatically
- publish a product
- change live Shopify data
