# Delta for Market Intelligence Strategic Insight

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Evidence-backed observations

Each observation MUST be traceable to evidence and include date, geography, confidence, and tags.

### Requirement: Observation immutability

Interpretation and synthesis MUST NOT overwrite the original observation record.

### Requirement: Pattern detection over history

The workflow MUST support identifying repeated signals, change, contradiction, and unknowns across observations.

### Requirement: Separate functional interpretations

Product, brand, commercial, and customer implications MUST remain independently retrievable.

### Requirement: Strategic synthesis

The system MUST synthesize what is known, what is uncertain, why it matters, and possible next actions.

### Requirement: Founder strategic decision

The workflow MUST pause for founder direction before a strategic action is considered committed.

Allowed outcomes:
- act
- investigate_further
- monitor
- archive

### Requirement: Durable knowledge history

Evidence, observations, insight, decision, and follow-up conditions MUST be persisted without overwriting prior history.

### Requirement: Recoverable execution

Research and synthesis stages MUST support retry after recoverable failures.
