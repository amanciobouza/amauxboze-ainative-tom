# Proposal

## Why

Amaux Bozé needs a persistent, evidence-based market intelligence process that separates source-backed observations from interpretation and strategic decisions.

The business process is defined, but there is not yet an executable workflow for collecting observations, detecting change, gathering functional interpretations, synthesizing strategic insight, obtaining founder direction, and persisting the resulting knowledge.

## What Changes

Implement Market Intelligence → Strategic Insight as a LangGraph workflow covering:

1. Research Question / Watch Topic
2. Evidence Collection
3. Observation Capture
4. Pattern / Change Detection
5. Functional Interpretation
6. Strategic Synthesis
7. Founder Decision
8. Knowledge Update

## Capabilities

### New Capability

- **market-intelligence-strategic-insight** — turn external evidence into traceable observations, interpretations, insights, and founder decisions.

## Dependencies

This change depends on the AI operating platform foundation and the existing multi-agent runtime.

## Impact

The workflow will coordinate Nora, Lucien, Élodie, Marc, Sophie, Elena, and Amancio.

Observations must remain immutable source-backed records; later interpretation must be stored separately.
